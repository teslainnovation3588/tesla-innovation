YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and transparently.

Our privacy commitment

TESLA Innovation does not sell, rent, or share customers' personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is used to provide our services, support customers, and improve their experience.

1. About TESLA Innovation

TESLA Innovation operates from the United States and provides vehicle sales, vehicle sourcing, purchase enquiries, and related customer services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Vehicle preferences and purchase enquiries.
  • Negotiation requests and proposed budgets.
  • Delivery country and delivery information you provide.
  • Account information when you register.
  • Order and customer-support information.
  • Technical information such as browser and device details, where collected by our website or service providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information only for legitimate business and service-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to vehicle enquiries.
  • Processing purchase and pre-order requests.
  • Managing customer accounts and orders.
  • Communicating about order progress and delivery.
  • Responding to customer-support requests.
  • Reviewing vehicle negotiation submissions.
  • Maintaining website functionality and security.
  • Improving our services and customer experience.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent customer personal information. We also do not share personal data with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Some information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate our website and deliver our services. These may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Database and authentication providers, including Supabase.
  • Communication and customer-support service providers.
  • Payment or delivery providers when required for a transaction.

These providers may process information on our behalf for the services they provide. We do not authorise them to use customer information for their own marketing purposes.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, valid legal process, or to protect legitimate rights and security.

5. Data Security

Protecting customer information is important to us. Our website uses HTTPS to help protect information transmitted between your browser and the website.

We aim to restrict access to customer information to authorised personnel who need it for legitimate business purposes.

We also work with technology providers that offer security features for website hosting, authentication, and data storage.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should protect their login credentials and contact us if they suspect unauthorised account access.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, manage customer relationships, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise handle it appropriately, subject to legal and operational requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing where applicable.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and its service providers may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to support essential website functions, account sessions, preferences, and website operation.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain storage technologies may affect website features, including account access.

9. Payments and Financial Information

Our website currently provides vehicle enquiries and purchase-related services. Online card payment processing is not currently presented as an active website feature.

If payment services are introduced, relevant payment information will be handled according to the payment provider's processes and the applicable terms disclosed at the time of payment.

10. International Customers

TESLA Innovation may receive enquiries from customers in different countries. Depending on the services requested, customer information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.

We aim to handle such information responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults involved in vehicle enquiries and purchases. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change.

Updated versions will be published on this page. We encourage customers to review this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding your personal information, please contact TESLA Innovation.
