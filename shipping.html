Our International Delivery Model

TESLA Innovation facilitates the sourcing and international shipment of vehicles from China directly to customers in their destination countries.

Customers can select their preferred vehicle, place an order and arrange delivery to their specified destination address, subject to vehicle availability and import eligibility.

Shipping Responsibilities

TESLA Innovation covers applicable agreed shipping, transportation and related charges incurred before the vehicle reaches the destination country.

Once the vehicle arrives in the destination country, the customer is responsible for applicable local charges, including:

  • Import duties and customs charges.
  • Applicable import taxes and VAT.
  • Customs clearance fees.
  • Vehicle inspection and registration fees.
  • Other applicable destination-country charges.

Customs & Import Requirements

Vehicle import regulations differ from country to country. Customers are responsible for ensuring that their selected vehicle can legally be imported, registered and used in their destination country.

Additional documentation, inspections, certifications or modifications may be required by local authorities.

Order & Delivery Process

  • Choose your preferred vehicle.
  • Submit your purchase order.
  • Confirm the vehicle price and payment arrangements.
  • Vehicle sourcing and shipping arrangements begin.
  • The vehicle is transported to the destination country.
  • The customer handles applicable destination charges.
  • Final customs clearance and delivery are arranged.

Delivery Information

Shipping schedules, transportation arrangements and delivery times depend on the vehicle, shipping route, customs procedures and destination country.

Customers should contact our sales team for an estimated delivery schedule and confirmation of applicable charges before completing an order.
