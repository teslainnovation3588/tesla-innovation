Global Currency Rules

Chinese vehicle brands: MG, Geely, GAC and BYD should normally be listed in Chinese Yuan (CNY).

Tesla: Use USD when the original price is quoted in US Dollars.

Important: Always select the actual currency of the price supplied to you. Do not convert the original amount before entering it.

International display conversion is handled by the vehicle inventory page. The original listing price remains stored in the vehicle record.
